The story

Marketing that has to pay for itself.

I'm Kajal Andani, a digital marketer, content creator, and educator. I've generated ₹1 Cr+ in revenue for clients across 60+ brands, and trained 400+ people in digital marketing.

I started out agency-side, and what I kept seeing bothered me: good brands buying retainers nobody could tie back to revenue. Reports full of impressions. Rankings that never turned into a single enquiry. Everyone was busy, and almost nobody could tell you what it earned.

So I built Kajal & Co. the other way round. We start from the number that matters to your business, then choose the website, search, and paid work that moves it. If a service won't move that number, we say so rather than sell it.

I still teach, because explaining this work plainly is the fastest way to find out whether you actually understand it. 400+ people later, I'm fairly sure the answer is yes.
