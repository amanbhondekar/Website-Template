Marketing that has to pay for itself.

Anyone can run an ad or launch a website. The hard part is knowing which one will actually bring you customers, and being honest when it won't.

I started Kajal & Co. because too many brands were paying for activity instead of outcomes: traffic nobody converted, rankings that never became enquiries, ads that looked busy and returned little.

So we work the other way round. We start from the number that matters to your business, then choose the website, search, and paid work that moves it. You get specialists in each area and one person accountable for the result.

I spent my first years agency-side watching good brands get sold retainers nobody could tie to revenue. I'd rather tell a founder we're not the right fit than take a budget I can't grow.
